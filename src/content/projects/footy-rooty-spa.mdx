## Overview

_Footy Rooty Spa_ is a massage and foot reflexology spa with two locations, in Roseville and Citrus Heights, California.
I built its website for a friend who runs the business.
New clients come to it to see what the spa offers and what it costs, catch the current deals, and book a visit at either store without picking up the phone.

## Features

- **Promotion-Led Home Page** - A full-screen photo carousel puts the spa's current deals first, each slide with its own promo tags, price card, and call to action

- **Services and Pricing** - The full treatment menu, from deep tissue and Swedish massage to foot reflexology, facials, and scalp therapy, with prices and prepaid packages

- **Memberships and Gift Cards** - Membership plans and gift cards, with what each includes and how to buy one in store

- **Online Booking** - Clients choose a store and book an appointment through an embedded Mindbody booking window, with the store's address one click away and its phone number as a fallback

- **Store Pages** - A photo gallery of each store, its hours and phone number, and an embedded map that opens in the visitor's maps app

## Responsibilities

- Built the whole site with **React**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Vite**, from the page layouts to deployment on **Vercel**

- Integrated **Mindbody** online booking for both stores, with guidance for choosing a store's therapist, a loading state, and a retry or call-the-store fallback when the booking window fails to load

- Kept every price and promotion in a single source, so the frequent price and deal changes are one-file edits that never leave two pages disagreeing

- Made the site fast on phones by generating responsive WebP versions of the photos, loading the hero photos on demand, and loading each page's code only when it is visited

- Made the site accessible, with a carousel, photo galleries, and mobile menu that all work from the keyboard, and color contrast checked across buttons, badges, and price labels

- Set up search and ads with per-page titles and descriptions, link previews, a not-found page, and a Google Ads conversion tag
