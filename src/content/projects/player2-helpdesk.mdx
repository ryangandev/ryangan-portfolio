_Player 2 Helpdesk_ is the support desk for [_Player 2_](https://player2app.com/), a social community for gamers.
A player reports a problem in the _Player 2_ app, the report arrives in the helpdesk as a ticket, and the technical operations staff at _Player 2_ work it there until it is resolved.

Three of us built the frontend, and I built most of it, from the first designs in **Figma** to the finished app.

## From report to resolution

I worked with the backend lead and their team to connect the helpdesk to the main _Player 2_ app, so a report filed there shows up as a ticket here.
Tickets update live: **server-sent events** carry each new ticket, chat message, and notification to the staff as it happens.

## The dashboard

The dashboard lists every ticket, filtered and sorted by status, agent, and date.
Each ticket opens to its details and a live chat with the player, where staff work it through to resolved.
A reports page charts how the desk is doing with **Recharts**, such as tickets per day, over any date range and filter.

## Under the hood

Staff register and sign in by magic link, reset their passwords, and see only what their role allows.
Global state lives in **Redux**, so a change made in one view shows in every other.
