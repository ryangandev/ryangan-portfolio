_Icon.io_ is a web platform for playing party games with friends, built around real-time updates: the lobby, rooms, chat, and play all happen live, broadcast to every player through sockets.
Its one game is _Draw & Guess_, where one player draws a word and the others guess it.

Three of us built it in three weeks, as the final project of a web development course.
I designed the interface in **Figma**, from the landing page where a player picks a name to the room list and the game, and built the real-time layer and the drawing board.

## Real Time, End to End

On the server, **Socket.io** broadcasts every change to the players it concerns: the lobby's room list, a room's players, chat messages, and each turn of the game.
In the browser, **React** state takes those broadcasts in, so every player's screen stays in step without a refresh.

## A Shared Drawing Board

The drawing board is a canvas with a color palette and brush settings.
Each stroke is broadcast as it is drawn, so the guessers watch the drawing appear as it happens.
